The Plant Nursery Management System is a web-based application developed to simplify the management of plant nursery operations and online plant sales. 
The system provides a centralized platform where customers can browse plants, view detailed plant information, place orders, and track their purchases. 
Administrators can manage plant categories, inventory, customer orders, reviews, complaints, and nursery activities through a dedicated dashboard.
The application aims to create an efficient and user-friendly digital environment for plant enthusiasts and nursery businesses.

Objectives-
To provide an online platform for showcasing a wide variety of plants.
To allow customers to browse plant details and care guides.
To enable online ordering and payment management.
To provide an admin dashboard for inventory and order management.
To manage customer reviews, ratings, and complaints.
To improve customer experience through easy access to plant-related information.
